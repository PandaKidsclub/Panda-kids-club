# Product Vision

Panda Kids Club is a premium cinematic children's streaming library. The finished product should feel like a major family entertainment brand: theatrical, colorful, dimensional, polished, welcoming, modern, and readable.

It must not become a generic corporate website, dashboard, school portal, ecommerce site, flat template, YouTube-style thumbnail grid, or simple Netflix clone.

The core browsing idea is:

```text
BROWSE -> HOVER/FOCUS -> THE STORY COMES ALIVE -> PLAY
```

Hero areas should eventually be video-led rather than static banners. Programme cards should eventually be able to select and promote a preview into a shared hero stage after intentional hover, keyboard focus, or touch selection. Stage 0 only prepares the architecture for that future behavior.

The visual direction is conceptual inspiration only. Do not copy proprietary layouts, source code, assets, icons, typefaces, or exact visual trade dress from other streaming products.
